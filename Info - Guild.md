# Guild Rules
- Be excellent to each other and people outside the guild
- No Racism/"Politics"
- No selling/discussing stuff that can't be sold through WoW (Gold, MLM, Crypto, ...)

# Questions/Issues/I'm on fire
If at any time during a raid there's something unclear or you feel like you have an urgent issue, feel free to whisper an officer or knight with your questions/problems. We're here to help and want everyone to end raids feeling happy!

# Enchants
Get them on every item you can, but let's be real +3 stats instead of +4 is fine ;). If your gear is not properly enchanted, all SRs on restricted items above will be void. This will be checked when trading the item so it's on you to get it in order.

Only open to members with the "Member" or higher rank.
Bank items: https://ozafy.net/lootorriot/

We will only keep "rare" raid loot in the GB (tier 1, scales, leather, books, recipes, idols, etc). Other stuff (coins, blues, greens, etc) will be auctioned for the GB's gold.
