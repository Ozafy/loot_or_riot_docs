# Attunement
Complete the quest : The Dread Citadel - Naxxramas in Light's hope chapel for the Argent Dawn:
- Honored: 60 Gold, 5x Arcane Crystal, 2x Nexus Crystal, 1x Righteous Orb
- Revered: 30 Gold, 2x Arcane Crystal, 1x Nexus Crystal
- Exalted: Free
## Requirements
- Make sure you are hit capped for your class
- Using proper raid spec (examples mages needs to go fire, and no more pve spec with a little bit of pvp talent here and there)
- Using proper rotations
- Bringing consumables for your class (see [here](https://discord.com/channels/1452686587568853245/1464382943379062939/1479092809511211029)). In the beginning I'd suggest at least 3x the recommended to account for wipes. The following recommendations already account for wipes:
- 5 Free Action Potions (15 for melee + tanks)
- 10 Greater nature protection
- 5 Greater fire protection
- 10 Greater frost protection
- 10 Greater shadow protection
- 5 Limited Invulnerability Potion (Melee+Mages) (+5 extra **ALL Warriors**)
- 5 Greater Stoneshield Potion (**Warrior Tanks**)
- 10 Elixir of Poison Resistance
- Disarm immunity (Warrior Tanks)
# Frost Resistance gear
- Melee: 105 unbuffed minimum (210 buffed recommended)
- Casters: 50-100 unbuffed (210 buffed recommended)
