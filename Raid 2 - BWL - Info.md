## Attunement
There is an attunement required for BWL. To get it, kill the "rare" Quartermaster outside UBRS (when in front of the BRS instance portal, follow the hall to the right, then the first left, he should now be in sight). Grab the item, accept the quest, finish UBRS and touch the orb behind Drakki.
## Critical Mechanics:
### Vaelastrasz (2nd boss):
- Burning Adrenaline: One person will become a "bomb" and explode. Run out of the group immediately! (Similar to the mechanic in MC). THIS ONE ACTUALLY KILLS.
### Nefarian (last boss) - Class Calls:
- Druids (**mages**): Stuck in catform. **MAGES** need to take over decursing the tank.
- Hunters: Your range weapon will **instantly** break the moment the call happens. Bring a backup, that way you can just swap **if** you get the call. If you don't have a proper backup, remove your weapon before every call.
- Mages (**priests** and **paladins**): You'll throw out random polys that need to be dispelled by **PRIESTS** and **PALADINS**. You can iceblock or you can hide in one of the doors, face the wall and think about what you've done.
- Paladins (**everyone**): You'll bop the boss. All **DPS** stops. If late enough in the fight and tank has enough threat, paladins can put up Judgement of Wisdom to let casters regen with wands. Use Divine shield before the call and BoP the mages when the dragons get resurrected!
- Priests: Stop all direct healing. Shield and renew only.
- Rogues (**tank**): Random TP + freeze. Evasion/Vanish. **TANK** reposition boss if safe or let them die.
- Warlocks (**mages**): You'll spawn infernals. Go stand on your own. **MAGES** freeze and dps them down (immune to fire).
- Warriors (**healers** and **dps**): Forced into zerker. **HEALERS** more heals as tanks will take 30% more damage. **DPS** watch threat as tanks will have 30% less threat.
## Specific Class Tasks:

### Tanks:
Get about 150 Fire res unbuffed, it'll help for Vael. If you're a warrior you also want to look at fury/prot instead of deep prot (with a shield + stoneshield potion at the ready if you're not confident in your gear).
### Rogues:
You will need to suppression devices using disarm trap. To do this you'll have to stealth around in between the mobs so any +stealth helps. If you can get your second spec into sub to grab the +stealth talents that'd be amazing. Otherwise there are +stealth boots available. One from your sunken temple class quest and one crafted: https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=20255/whisperwalk-boots and https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=8197/nightscape-boots
### Druids:
Two druids will be assigned to sleep one dragon each (Hibernate). Please put this spell on your bars! Hibernate must be spammed to interrupt the dragons' fire casts. If not, the whole raid takes massive damage. Pro Tip: For the first pack, use your instant-cast spell combo to Sleep the target immediately, giving you time to position yourself.

## What do we expect for BWL?
- Proper Gear: No empty slots or useless trinkets (carrot on a stick, ...). If you're struggling to fill a specific slot, ask for help in #gearing-advice . Please aim for Pre-Raid BiS.
- Enchants: Get your gear enchanted
- World Buffs: Not required, but they help tremendously, grab as many as you easily can. 3 Will get you an extra SR.
- Flasks: Not required, but as with WBs they do help tremendously. If you can afford it, please feel free to bring some. You can replace one WB with a flask and still get an extra SR.
