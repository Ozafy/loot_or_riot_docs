## Requirements
- Make sure you are hit capped for your class
- Using proper raid spec (examples mages needs to go fire, and no more pve spec with a little bit of pvp talent here and there)
- Using proper rotations
- Bringing the required consumables for your class (see below)
- 1 Greater arcane protection
- 5 Greater nature protection
- 5 Greater shadow protection
 On top of this we need warlocks to be able to tank Twins ([Guide](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/twin-emperors-warlock-tanking)).
